One season per program type in 2027. These dates are proposals, not verified school calendars. Concert band and choir use a spring campaign; adjust them to match your plan.

Monthly headcounts cover the busiest weekday in a five-day, Monday–Friday workweek. No work is scheduled on weekends. Staffing = total minutes for sales demos, launch sessions, tickets, and live chat ÷ available working minutes per person, rounded up. Sales, campaign launch, live-chat, and support time are combined before rounding up, so one shared team can flex between roles and serve successive seasons. Email outreach is scheduled one or two weeks before its demos, including work in late 2026. Each successful program needs one launch demo per fundraiser with a nonzero goal. Launch demos are spread across season weekdays until campaign dates are planned. Live chat covers every signup during the first 28 calendar days of its season, spread across weekdays. Staffing covers both types of demos, live chat, and support; email preparation and follow-ups are not included.

Assumes every booked demo happens and each successful program generates its tickets during one season. New-customer demos run before the season; campaign launch demos and tickets run during it. All are spread evenly over Monday–Friday; holidays and within-season spikes are not modeled. Headcounts cover the added programs only, excluding existing workload, outreach time, unsuccessful-program tickets, and absences. They are capacity estimates, not hiring recommendations.
