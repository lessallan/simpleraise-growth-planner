Account-creation targets are spread across the demo window; financial-success targets are placed at season end. These are planning targets, not measured results. Monthly headcounts cover the busiest weekday in a five-day, Monday–Friday workweek. No work is scheduled on weekends. Staffing = total minutes for sales demos, launch sessions, tickets, and live chat ÷ available working minutes per person, rounded up. Sales, campaign launch, live-chat, and support time are combined before rounding up, so one shared team can flex between roles and serve successive seasons. Initial outreach starts before each planned demo; follow-ups use the chosen interval, with a booking buffer after the final email, including work in late 2026. Email counts are maximums: early bookings and opt-outs reduce actual sends. Each successful program needs one launch demo per fundraiser with a nonzero goal. Launch demos are spread across season weekdays until campaign dates are planned. Unplanned live chats use the separate successful-program and unsuccessful-account estimates, spread across weekdays in the first 28 calendar days of each season. Staffing covers both types of demos, live chat, and support; email preparation and follow-ups are not included.

Assumes every booked demo happens and each account generates its estimated tickets during one season. New-customer demos run before the season; campaign launch demos and tickets run during it. Demos and service work are spread evenly over Monday–Friday; drip emails follow their scheduled weekday sequence. Holidays and within-season spikes are not modeled. Headcounts cover the added programs only, excluding existing workload, outreach time and absences. They are capacity estimates, not hiring recommendations.
